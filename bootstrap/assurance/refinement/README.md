# Cross-rung refinement assurance

This owner contains untrusted obligation reconstruction and gates that relate
programs or meaning from one bootstrap layer to artifacts or observations at
another. Placement does not make an encoder trusted: canonical meaning,
artifact fingerprints, lower-rooted checking, and negative controls remain the
authority.

- [`beta/`](beta/) reconstructs the Beta-source/Alpha-artifact edge. It owns
  both symbolic derivations, their curated and generated corpora, differential
  pins, and kernel-checked equivalence gates.
- [`omega0/`](omega0/) reconstructs meaning-route and native-result claims for
  the first-Omega work, then replays generated certificates across the checker
  implementations.

Rung-local meaning and conformance stay with their rung. Alpha therefore owns
its written VM semantics, native seeds, executable reference, opcode tests,
seed fuzzing, and assembler. Omega0 owns its meaning elaborator, compiler
profiles/source, bundle and artifact conformance, convergence, and ordinary
meaning-route gates.
