# L6 checker contracts

## Access-policy evidence

The access-policy evaluator needs to know whether a selected constructor establishes its result. `check_record_constructor` takes the immutable Psi unit containing the executable body and its exact declaration identity. The L5 producer test prepares and lowers the shipped toolchain as a verification unit. It checks that the constructor has a body and that the nine catalog-supplied declarations have their separate identities. [Producer test](../05_prepared-trees-to-terminal-psi/tests/obligation_library.rs)

The proof subject is the one closed machine body the unit publishes for that declaration. `new_plan<'t>(schema: @TypeSchema<'t>, issuance: Issuance<'t>) -> AccessPlan<'t>` names its lifetime only inside records the toolchain lowers, so the toolchain publishes one executable body for every `'t` and no open template. A unit that also carries a template, a catalog entry, a theorem or a trait requirement under the same declaration has more than one candidate, and the checker refuses it instead of choosing. [Closed constructor body](../05_prepared-trees-to-terminal-psi/tests/policy_constructor.rs)

The body's type rows erase lifetime arguments. The checker first reads each formal's and the result's declared type back from its row and the machine's region slots: every lifetime binder of the record needs exactly one region, and a stored record's binders come from its field's `aggregate_lifetimes`. A field can instead store a shared reference to such a record, as `storage: @'t Bytes<'t>` does. Its row names the record it refers to, `lifetime` is the binder the reference is held at, and the same list fills the referent's binders; a row with the list and no `lifetime` is refused, because nothing says where that reference is held. Copying such a field copies the reference. The checker requires the referent to be a declared record at those binders and reads none of its fields, so the copy claims no borrow held behind the reference. [Stored references](tests/stored_references.rs) It then follows the body operation by operation. A copy must read the referent of a shared formal, and a whole move must take an owned formal. Every other operation must form a zero: a zero constant, the first case of a sum with a zero payload, a fixed array filled from zeros, a zeroed record. The last operation establishes the returned record from those results in declaration order, each used once, and the body returns it with no cleanup. The checker then judges the returned application, nested data requirements, ownership and every declared guarantee against those origins. The resulting `CheckedRecordConstructor` borrows the defining unit and its body, and identifies the declared formals, the exact inputs and the result to which the proof applies. The mutation tests change one fact of the body or of the unit's data at a time, including a region slot, a non-zero count, a slot's second case and a nested requirement that rejects zero. [Checker](src/lib.rs) · [Body rules](src/constructor_body.rs) · [Storage rules](src/storage.rs) · [Checks against the real constructor](tests/constructor.rs)

The caller-side judgment first checks the selected declaration, its conformance to the defining trait, the schema loan, the issuance case and the returned constructor call. It retains the identity of the empty route field for `Only { routes: [] }` and `Only { .. }`. [Selected policy calls](tests/policy_call.rs)

`check_closed_policy_call` then checks the actual lowered caller in the same unit as the constructor. The executable call must use the selected machine ID, region, schema formal and issuance value, and return the constructor result. The proof borrows both bodies. Substituting another unit, redirecting a call, changing a payload or introducing an extra effect invalidates the proof. The interpreter accepts this pair for policy execution. [Closed caller judgment](src/closed_policy_call.rs) · [Caller mutations](tests/closed_policy_call.rs) · [Metered execution](../../interpreter/tests/access_policies.rs)

At the actual constructor occurrence, `check_policy_construction` compares the frozen field inputs with the checked caller and constructor. The schema field receives the original immutable byte storage from the selected invocation. The issuance field receives its checked case and payload. Every remaining field must agree with its proven typed zero. The result retains the original package key, subject revision, selection site and construction site for the requesting preparation. A foreign occurrence, different copied value or field reordering refuses. [Concrete construction checks](tests/policy_call.rs)

The interpreter owns execution under the root's resource ceilings. Preparation validates the returned data against the requested schema and region. Lowering consumes the bound answer when publishing domain issuers. Additional callable routes, restrictions and recursive execution require their own evidence. [Invocation admission](../../../wiki/spec/language/evaluation.md#invocation-admission) · [Access-plan validation](../../../wiki/spec/resources/access.md#plans-and-admitted-supply)

## Mixed scalar qualification casts

`check_scalar_replacements` checks closed machine bodies containing a mixed scalar transfer. The caller supplies one immutable lowered package. The checker compares the argument and block parameter types independently of the jump marker, keeps the same payload carrier, checks the exact added atoms and their issuers, and refuses removal with an outstanding cleanup obligation. Parent memberships must survive the cast or be independently established on that argument. Each added predicate must hold before the destination invariant becomes available. A successful judgment covers these transfers under the machine's entry contract.

For `distance as -Km +Positive`, a signed payload of 7 establishes `self > 0`. A payload of −7 fails that predicate and can gain a predicate-free `Degrees`. The checker can also use a matching scalar requirement on the actual formal, including a requirement inherited from its incoming domain. It tracks exact literals, checked negation, and facts preserved across qualification jumps. A branch that compares that payload with an integer constant supplies its comparison or its negation on the corresponding edge. At a control-flow join it keeps facts established on every incoming path. The current proof fragment evaluates integer and Boolean predicate operations and substitutes closed integer or Boolean domain indices. A proof requiring another operation, a relation between different values, or induction around a loop remains open. [Checker and mutation tests](tests/scalar_replacements.rs)

## Computed integer ranks

The driver supplies an immutable lowered package and the exact two natural ranking views from its selected toolchain. `check_computed_ranks` visits each closed call component containing a computed descending rank. It validates the view identity and carrier, instantiates the expression over the machine's own formals, and checks every intermediate under its selected integer policy. A written rank range constrains the result and supplies no entry premise. For `K + n` with `K = 3u64`, the entry must establish `n <= 18446744073709551612`. The operation's source occurrence owns the diagnostic. [Ranking](../../../wiki/spec/language/termination.md#ranking) · [Checked source and Psi mutations](tests/computed_ranks.rs)

The checker reconstructs local calls from Psi and compares each recursive claim against the target's rank on the actual arguments. Exact affine equations, integer intervals, comparisons with literal bounds, and the facts shared at control-flow joins can justify formation and descent. Comparisons use the point of invocation. The call must also preserve any input bound used from the callee's entry contract. Removing all proven strict edges must leave an acyclic call graph. A wrapping subtraction that decreases after wraparound and a saturating subtraction that stays at zero can thus receive different verdicts on the same call. [Control flow](../../../wiki/spec/terminal-psi/control_flow.md#safety-and-progress)

Success covers rank formation and recursive comparisons for the inspected closed components. Other termination premises, template proofs, CFG backedge induction and recursive cleanup calls require separate evidence. The numeric proof fragment conservatively reports insufficient evidence for facts it cannot derive. It materializes auxiliary integer bounds up to 65,536 bits; the two admitted views themselves return `u64`.

## Build continuation work

A parked local call names a machine and a call site in one immutable lowered unit. The checker verifies the published callee and the instruction or edge that follows normal completion. A terminal call forwards a normal return. A second edge with the same identity makes the location ambiguous. Checking this location leaves execution admission, correspondence with a later unit and restoration of the live activation as separate judgments. [Local call judgment](src/suspension_control.rs) · [Changed targets, successors and edge identities](tests/suspension_control.rs)

Once a dependency has been admitted, its ordinary machines may supply values. For straight-line scalar entries the checker follows local calls, checks their operands and results, and checks literal range, integer comparisons and scalar returns. Recursion, storage, host operations and additional proof obligations require further judgments. The interpreter receives the exact checked unit and bodies and runs them on their actual arguments under the invocation ceilings. [Scalar entry judgment](src/scalar_return.rs) · [Metered imported calls](../../interpreter/tests/scalar_return.rs)
