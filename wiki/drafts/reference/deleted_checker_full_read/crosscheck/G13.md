## Summary

I checked 110 of the 113 inventory rows. I skipped rows 27, 36 and 113 as the old code's internal bookkeeping (budgets, an optimization, caches).

- **Enforced:** 49
- **Refused wholesale:** 26
- **Not found:** 4
- **Unclear:** 23

Paths are relative to `source-rust/subsystems/compiler/`. `05/` means `source-to-psi/pipeline/05_prepared-typed-trees-to-lowered-program/src/`, and `06/` means `source-to-psi/pipeline/06_lowered-program-to-checked-program/src/`. `sem/` means `psi/semantics/src/`.

Recasts are now judged after lowering. Stage 05 refuses every exclusive recast (`05/read/casts.rs:39-42`). Psi semantics then judges shared recasts:
- equal scalar width (`psi/types/representation_views/scalars.rs:175-187`);
- tiling and count fit (`06/places/byte_views.rs:586-735`);
- every target layout alternative (`sem/references/representation_geometry.rs:143-166`).

Fact-carrying targets are refused instead of being checked by implication. Any target with `requires`, a scalar restriction, qualifications or atomic fields is not "total" and is refused (`representation_geometry.rs:94-101, 197-245`). That is stricter than the old rule, so it is safe. Nonzero byte offsets are refused too (`06/places/loans.rs:1157-1165`).

Reference-result custody moved into the descriptor replay in `sem/descriptors/replay/engine.rs` and `sem/references/`. It fails closed: recursion through returned references is refused (`engine.rs:3304-3310`).

Trait checking leans on conformance witnesses, which the checker proves (`06/verdict.rs:1595-1630`). Several trait features are now refused before checking:
- `dyn` is rejected by the parser;
- `via` satisfiers;
- `refines`;
- named propositions;
- encoded wire schemas.

The two findings that matter most:
1. **Type-application arity.** At `source-to-psi/pipeline/03_symbol-resolved-trees-to-typed-trees/src/type_reference.rs:908-929`, an application whose argument count differs from the declaration's parameter count is skipped with `continue`, with no diagnostic. I found no other arity check for ordinary type, trait-bound or alias applications.
2. **Wire schemas without an `encoding`.** These are not refused (`05/read/subjects.rs:640-647` filters on `schema.encoding.is_some()`), and nothing checks their field-number rules.
