## Summary

Of 73 inventory rows, 5 are the old code's bookkeeping and were skipped (R29 call-row mismatch, R30 boundary-edge record, R68 nominal dependency list, R72 product pruning, R73 architecture test). Of the remaining 68: **Enforced 48, Refused wholesale 4, Not found 4, Unclear 6, Not looked up 6.**

The current checker replaces the old fact store with facts about SSA values. A store never removes a fact; a read gets an equation only from the one store that reaches it on every path, found by one overlap relation (`S06/src/places.rs:201`) and one reaching walk (`S06/src/places.rs:877-899`, `3638`). Most of the invalidation rules R1-R7, R15, R33, R47 and R53 therefore hold by construction rather than by a dedicated check, and the old per-placement copies have no counterpart. Note that the stage 05 README's "subset" section is out of date: the tree now serves reference locals, stored references, byte views, arrays, atomics, declared domains, cleanup machines and floats. I judged from the code, not that section.

The four "not found" rows all concern old inference that made the checker accept more programs: coinductive postconditions over recursion (R18), a variant tag inferred from a callee body (R19, the non-scalar part), narrowing a dynamic call to one implementation (R28), and transport of byte classes through concatenation (R52, that part only). With these gone the checker refuses more programs; none of the four looks like a check that was lost and now lets bad programs through. The four wholesale refusals are multi-state machines (R21), case-level `requires` (R38), quotient data (R71), and the case proved by excluding every other case (R36). The new checker refuses R36 at each payload read that needs it, not at the declaration.
