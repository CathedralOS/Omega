# Group G11b: validation contract prover, second half (structural judge, storage effects, terms, entailment driver)

All citations are to `contract_entailment.rs`, written `CE:line`. Slices 104-109 cover CE:46181-92926 (46,746 lines). Six reports read in full.

## What this code was

This is the `StructuralJudge`, the old checker's own theorem prover for `requires`/`ensures`, and the function that drives it for each machine (`validate_machine_contract_entailment`, CE:92193-92836). The judge holds, for one execution path, a bag of equations, case facts, identities, conditional guarantees, integer premises and storage observations, and answers `Proven`, `Refuted` or `Unknown` (CE:79980-79985). It enumerates paths through state bodies by cloning itself at every guard, match arm, `and`/`or` and call outcome (CE:46181-47317, CE:65709-66530). Inside it sit a polynomial integer prover with a Boolean case splitter, an IEEE float reasoner, a decision procedure for the identity relation `Id`, ring and semiring rearrangement licensed by trait conformances, a versioned model of storage, a symbolic interpreter that unfolds callee bodies, and a closed-world analysis that gives private nested machines entry facts from their callers. Three translators turn source expressions into the judge's term language `StructuralTerm`, whose variables, fields, projection paths and machine names are strings (CE:80503-80601). Only the driver and the rank functions produce text a user can see; every other refusal is `None`, `Unknown` or a trace line. Nothing in the range builds or checks a proof object.

## Mechanisms

- **Path enumeration by cloning the judge.** `recognize_structural_state_leaves_from` (CE:46181-47317) and `evaluate_normal_paths` (CE:65577-66530) return one judge per path; each operand multiplies the list. The first has no numeric budget, only path-membership guards; the second stops at depth 64 (CE:65821). Pure outcomes are rejoined (R6). Serves every contract proof.
- **Demand and stop protocol.** One walker answers five demands and three stop points (CE:64513-64597), so other modules can ask for "the judge just before this operation" (CE:64948-65181). Prefix visitors receive the judge at a statement (CE:87006-87066).
- **Equation store.** Equations are oriented into substitutions or rewrites with occurs checks, depth 32 (CE:52729-52900); the `RewriteTable` indexes entries by a hash of up to four nested levels and gives each one of three meanings (CE:80603-81096).
- **Resolver.** Rewrites a term to normal form using the table, substitutions, folding, float truth and call unfolding; 32 steps and depth 32, returning the partly rewritten term with no signal when either runs out (CE:83175-83193, 83542).
- **Call unfolding and check-time evaluation.** A symbolic interpreter over a single-state callee with locals, decided guards and tail calls, depth 32 (CE:83747-84007).
- **Integer projection.** Terms become polynomials over named atoms; operations start unadmitted; premises are installed until nothing new is admitted (CE:53823-54161, 55618-55849). Depth 64. A case splitter builds disjunctive normal forms with budgets 64 nodes, 32 worlds, 64 comparisons (CE:54556-54759). A fast path of about 270 lines of early exits can answer "not proven" finally (CE:54977-55250).
- **`Id` decision.** Syntactic identity, normalization, disjointness, congruence, then transitive search; work limit 8192, depth 32 or 64, 64 forms per side (CE:49689, 49799-50046). A second implementation groups identities into connected components and picks the cheapest representative (CE:56453-56647).
- **Licensed algebra.** Multiset comparison, then breadth-first hypothesis exchange, depth 2, frontier 64; polynomial normal form capped at 64 monomials (CE:47984-48146). Licenses come from a conformance table joined on application, carrier and operation (CE:57639-57682, 58393-58641).
- **Float worlds.** Premises expand to at most 32 worlds of 64 predicates; a bound is proven when every world implies it (CE:79600-79706).
- **Versioned storage observations.** Each observation has a coordinate (root symbol plus field, case and index segments), entry and current terms, anchors and a storage version; validity is asked of the storage graph per read, or approximated by wholesale retirement when no graph exists (CE:73928-74616, 76850-76989). A hash-indexed container holds them (CE:75692-75888).
- **Call contract instantiation.** `bound_contract_term` translates contract expressions under symbol bindings, depth 64, consulting the judge as it goes (CE:71084-71570); `capture_guarantees` sorts results into equations, identities and conditional guarantees by cloning the judge per branch (CE:72637-72976).
- **Body summaries.** Returned-field templates with a cycle-aware memo (CE:69700-69937); the constant-Boolean body walk (CE:72013-72191).
- **Private arrivals.** A whole-program index of nested machines and call sites, cycle-entry selection, strongly connected components, and progressive caller frontiers with no numeric budget (CE:85434-86340).
- **Rank comparison.** A rank is saved as a polynomial denotation with named atoms, re-instantiated at the arrival and compared lexicographically (CE:53490-53683).
- **Memo caches.** An 8-entry cache of prepared premises (CE:53863-53895); an 8,192-entry query cache keyed by term fingerprint and the allocation addresses of 20 fact tables (CE:67574-67793); process-wide memos for reachability, returned fields and application carriers (CE:47438-47530, 69820-69826, 71817-71821).
- **Entailment cascade.** Per `ensures`: indexed mathematical contract, verbatim `requires`, content conservation, structural judgment with per-arm induction, then result bounds and inductive transitions, or arithmetic and collection predicates (CE:92193-92836).

## Patterns not seen before

1. **Refutation is as trusted as proof.** Seven places turn the judge's own "contradictory" flag into acceptance (R5). The flag is set by distinct constructors, distinct integers, a refuted invariant case and conflicting type-atom names (CE:52810-52812, 52895-52897, 52233-52241, 52922-52924). Any wrong refutation therefore accepts everything on that arm; no report saw a diagnostic for unsatisfiable `requires`.
2. **A callee's body leaks facts it never declared.** Six mechanisms: body walk in place of contract (R31), returned-field templates, struct-literal projection and nominal receipts (R32), constant-Boolean bodies (R24), unfolding (R34), plus caller-derived entry facts in the other direction (R116). A rebuilt compiler must decide which of these are language rules.
3. **One term type, two equalities, and Debug text as a third.** `==` erases observed type and descriptor; `same_resolution_payload` keeps them (CE:81283-81597). Three slices found decision sites using the weaker one. Debug text is a sort key at 12 sites (CE:47994-48227), an atom name (CE:58983), the observation identity (CE:81257-81260) and a former cache key (CE:68730-68735).
4. **`Opaque` strings as a hidden identity channel.** Unformed names and calls become `Opaque(display text)` (CE:91356, 91473), unobserved things get fixed strings (CE:57425, 59739, 67185, 89063), equality is by text (CE:81556), and a prefix match turns the text back into a projection (CE:84908-84972). Visible only across slices 104, 105, 108 and 109.
5. **Silent refusals.** Reasons exist only as trace output: about 80 `eprintln!` sites (10 in 104, 13 in 105, 3 in 106, 17 plus a 390-line trace module in 107, about 25 in 108, 14 in 109). Slice 107 computes about 40 reason strings and discards some (CE:75359). User text exists only for ranks (CE:53503-53747) and the driver (CE:92654-92833).
6. **Budgets that end in `Unknown` with no message.** Depth 32 in at least 20 places, 64 in 8, 128 once; 32 worlds; 64 monomials; 8192 identity work; reference peeling 4 (CE:78679). A program just past a limit is refused without being told why.
7. **Answers depend on unrelated context.** Fast-path exits keyed on whether any rewrite exists (CE:55162-55245), folding blocked by observation requests, translation that consults the judge, caches shared between cloned judges, and a comment warning that a trace query "could change the observed judgment" (CE:76481-76482).
8. **The same fact kept in parallel regimes.** Storage with a graph versus without (opposite defaults); runtime-body, specification and case-facts-only modes that switch rules off (CE:54398-54400, 72202-72208, 84996-84998); value preservation skipping scope entry (CE:57378-57387).
9. **Fresh values built from invalid handles plus a counter** at seven sites (CE:46519-46532, 60398, 61672, 66511-66521, 73601-73610, 74935-74943, 78875-78887); the last forgot the counter.
10. **Comments promising what code does not do**, five times: "by SHAPE, not by name" (CE:57767), no circular licensing (CE:58270-58274), variable sides become substitutions (CE:52654-52655), display-only terms prove nothing (CE:91088-91090), numeric equality gives `Id` no rule (CE:49781-49782).
11. **Tests guard the plumbing, not the rules.** Of 57 tests, 22 pin the query cache, 6 a container, 5 trace formatting, 4 payload equality; none runs the path evaluator, guarantee capture, storage rules, private arrivals or the driver on a program.
12. **Rules decided far from their owner.** Rank rules sit in three modules (CE:53490-53750, 70071-70147, 73809-73925); induction admission in three (R41); record eta in two (R51).

## What a rebuilt compiler must still do

- Prove `ensures` from `requires` over every path of a machine body, refining facts at guards, matches, `is` tests and short-circuit operators (R1, R9-R16, R18-R20).
- Keep positive, negative and exhaustive case knowledge about data values, including through stores to variant payloads (R42-R47, R13).
- Give a caller a callee's `ensures` only after proving its `requires` at that call, with guarantees that hold only under stated conditions kept conditional (R23, R25-R27, R19).
- Decide which facts a callee's body may give callers beyond its declared contract, and under what visibility rule (R24, R31, R32, R34, R116).
- Honour citations: proof calls that write nothing establish their guarantees in authored order (R21, R22, R40).
- Admit an induction hypothesis for a recursive call only when its `requires` hold and its rank strictly decreases (R41, R114, R115).
- Compare termination ranks lexicographically with proved lower bounds and carrier fit (R114, R115).
- Assume a data type's and variant's `requires`, domain rows and value indices for values that provably have that type, and withhold them for pending recasts (R63-R70).
- Reason about integers with carrier bounds, overflow-policy admission, linear and polynomial facts, Boolean case splits, fits checks and per-policy folding (R71, R74-R81).
- Track narrowed layout fields, casts, recasts and bitwise complement exactly (R73, R82).
- Reason about floats by IEEE meaning, including NaN and bit-pattern identity (R83, R84, R49, R50).
- Decide `Id` by congruence, constructor injectivity and disjointness, record eta and transitivity, never reading through a reference (R48-R56).
- Let a user trait earn ring or semiring rearrangement only through checked conformance, with no circular licensing (R85-R90).
- Invalidate facts about storage on writes by exact field path, keep disjoint places, and account for cleanup hooks that write (R91-R96).
- Know when a place still holds its entry value and when a scalar may be read through a reference (R97-R99, R102).
- Carry storage facts across call entry and return only when the write frame proves them preserved (R29, R100).
- Allow only total, pure calls as values in propositions, and unfold or evaluate them within stated limits (R33-R35, R39).
- Intersect guarantees across the members of a target-family interface (R28).
- Give lengths of fixed arrays, strings and windows, and element equations for literals (R106, R107, R72).
- Define zero values and which omitted struct fields may take them (R108-R110).
- Derive type-parameter equations from case guards and static type predicates (R62, R117).
- Report refuted and unprovable `ensures` distinctly, veto refutable admitted claims, and report unsatisfiable `requires` instead of accepting everything (R5, R120-R123).
- Give placement calls their result geometry from declarations, not from hard-coded names (R38).

## Notes the authors left

- Known gap, in a diagnostic: proof-only data is something "which no entailment tier judges yet -- accepting it would certify an unproven structural claim" (CE:92715-92719).
- Induction soundness is delegated: "sound because validate_proof_machine_recursion refuses non-descending self-calls in this same diagnostics batch" (CE:92309-92321); "Register only the exact recursive call after its entry requirements and decrease have been checked" (CE:57183-57187).
- Data invariants are trusted at use: "Every value of a data type satisfies its `requires` (construction and every write are checked against the default domain) ... admitting the predicate here would let the cast establish itself" (CE:62128-62137).
- Written preconditions close a machine: "Even `requires true` cannot borrow a stronger condition from callers" (CE:85659-85661); a top-level machine "has no root here on purpose" (CE:85731-85734).
- Licenses: "a carrier EARNS ring canonicalization over an op through EXPLICIT conformance, never scope-sniffing ... (whose proofs rung B already machine-checked against the declared laws)" (CE:57763-57771); "NO CIRCULAR LICENSING" (CE:58270-58274); "Frontier-capped BFS -- over-refusal past the cap, never unsound" (CE:48010-48011).
- `Id` is not equality: "Executable numeric equality, including IEEE signed-zero equality, supplies no rule in this reader" (CE:49781-49782); "forming an Id supplies no proof of it" (CE:52623-52625); "reading equal referent contents never identifies their descriptors" (CE:89904-89906).
- Specification calls: "A proposition may use a call as a value term only when the call is total and pure (domains.md ... contracts.md ...)" (CE:84121-84124).
- Storage: "Writes retire live storage observations, not completed value identities ... Without a graph ... other writes remain opaque" (CE:72979-72990); "an unresolved place may have changed, so it is invalidated and must be observed again" (CE:74350-74353); the displaced value's "`dropped by` hook may write through exclusive references it held" (CE:78206-78211).
- Identity by spelling was a stated worry: "The arm's descriptor is the value this walk binds to the parameter, not its spelling" (CE:66796-66799); "Debug provides deterministic grouping only; equality always checks the complete payload" (CE:47940-47941).
- Cost drove design: conditional guarantees mean "assuming each condition in a copy of the judge and re-examining everything already known there" (CE:71701-71704); `arm_premises` "used to rebuild this judge at each call site ... n^2 admissions" (CE:87235-87253).
- Disjunction: "everything the right operand publishes holds only where the left one is false" (CE:72821-72829).
