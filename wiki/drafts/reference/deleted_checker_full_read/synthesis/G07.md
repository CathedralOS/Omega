## What this code was

The flow builder walked every statement, expression, call, guard, transition and exit of every machine in evaluation order. For each program point it recorded which facts and borrow constraints held there (057 `flow.rs:1-31`; 060 `flow_expression.rs:1-16`). It worked out what each write, call and loan expiry changed (058, 062), what a state could assume when control arrived there (064 fsv:2851-2966), and which facts a `let` or assignment re-created under the destination path (065 `flow_transfers.rs:1-19`). It also prepared ownership move events (063 `flow_ownership.rs`), cleanup plans for dying values (058 `flow.rs:11295-12317`), loan handbacks (063 `flow_retained_borrows.rs`) and interprocedural summaries: mutation summaries (062 `flow_mutation.rs:2713-3235`), returned predicates (057 `flow.rs:1060-1319`) and a symbolic walk of a callee's body (065 `flow_transfers.rs:2025-2513`). A whole-group fixed point drove all of this, run in parallel and supported by record-and-replay (057 `flow.rs:235-911`; 064 fsv:3598-3773). The builder almost never refused a program. It decided only whether a fact row existed, and consumers in other stages gave the yes/no. Its only diagnostics were two cleanup errors (058 `flow.rs:11634, 11668`), three case-observation errors (062 `flow_observations.rs:107-125`), five owned-operand errors (063 `flow_ownership.rs:246-286`), and the product-pruning and quotient errors in `products.rs` (065).

## Mechanisms

- **Group fixed point** (057 `flow.rs:532-603`). Each sweep restarts from the declaration and proof facts. The pass bound starts at `2 + Σ(1 + 2·params)` and grows during the loop. If the group does not settle, it is rebuilt once with all incoming values unknown. A union-find groups the machines (709-893).
- **Record and replay** (057 `flow.rs:402-693`; 064 fsv:3598-3773). Independent parts converge alone on rayon workers and record every delivery. `ReplayGuard` forces a serial rebuild when a replay would differ. This exists for build speed, and one program shape is named as the motivation ("each hosted target's `plan` body", 057 `flow.rs:706-707`).
- **Returned-predicate greatest fixed point** (057 `flow.rs:1060-1128`). Starts from "everything holds" and keeps only the rows a complete pass reproduces. This is coinduction over recursion, and termination is ranked elsewhere.
- **State-arrival join with widening** (064 fsv:702-1435). Parameters widen after 4 growths to program literals. Fields keep a per-edge delivery store, widen after 4 growths and freeze after 8 shrinks. The thresholds are the program's compared integer literals. Comments give the reason: "hundreds of thousands of sweeps" (fsv:706-716) and "183 passes" (fsv:1190-1200).
- **Mutation summary fixed point** (062 `flow_mutation.rs:2713-3235`). Runs in parallel with rayon and is incremental through `seen_writes`. It has no iteration budget, and an incomplete summary is never recomputed.
- **Origin dataflow and backward value traces** (057 `flow.rs:2449-2567`, no bound; 058 `flow.rs:9332-9513`, 16 literal hops; 063 `flow_reference_places.rs:146-532`, depth 16).
- **Storage snapshot chain** (057 `flow.rs:1454-1611`; 061 `flow_expression.rs:10731-10788`). Persistent `Writes`/`Store`/`Join` nodes with an address-resolution worklist limited to depth 64 (057 `flow.rs:1827-1862`).
- **Boolean condition DAG** (057 `flow.rs:3638-3941`). Hash-consed, with Shannon `restrict` and memoised `covers`/`possible`. Every lookup is a linear scan.
- **Interprocedural walkers with no budget**: the symbolic callee walk (065 `flow_transfers.rs:2025-2513`), which forks on unknown guards without a memo table and so can be exponential; the selector-origin search (058 `flow.rs:12384-12575`; 060 `flow_expression.rs:3837-3918`); the captured-value projection (063 `flow_operand_values.rs:740-976`, no cycle guard visible).
- **Depth caps**: 64 (057), 32 place depth (058 `flow.rs:8443`), 16 (058, 063), 8 selector fields (060 `flow_expression.rs:3804`), 128 operand chain (062 `flow_mutation.rs:2076`).
- **Cleanup structural walk** (058 `flow.rs:11737-12160`): descends the type, partitions arrays at removed indexes, and produces nominal or symbolic leaves.
- **Region algebra** (062 `flow_mutation.rs:2306-2469`): write footprints are declared storage, resident content, nonresident content or unresolved content.

## Patterns not seen before

- **Refusal by absence.** Eight of nine slices issue no diagnostic; about 10 user diagnostics come from roughly 57,000 lines. Every rule here works by a missing fact that some distant consumer then misses. 060 (Surprise 1) and 061 (Surprise 1) say a reader cannot learn from the file what a program is refused for.
- **Silent reasons.** Reasons for withholding a fact are computed and then printed only under trace flags: the nine `ReferenceProjectionRefusal` reasons (060 `flow_expression.rs:2264-2317`), the 35 named gates (062 `flow_observations.rs:248-478`), the `CHECKSITE`/`CALLWRITE`/`STRUCT` traces (062, 063 `flow_place.rs:923-934`), `eprintln!` in hot paths (057 5458; 061 9960). Twelve trace sites appear in 060 alone.
- **Copies that disagree.** At least eight pairs compute one judgment two ways with different answers: R1 (058), R25 (060), R57 (063), the call argument order (061), the operator missing place (057), parameter versus field widening (064), the alias-closure fallback (059 vs 062), the lanes reading different snapshots (065 `flow_transfers.rs:4117` vs 4157).
- **Comments contradicting code.** About 14: 057 4302-4304; 058 7464, 9038; 059 897-899, 265-271; 061 10941; 062 4711, 2541, `flow_operand_contexts.rs:11-14`; 063 `flow_reference_places.rs:22-23`, `flow_place.rs:1859`; 064 fsv:1437; 065 1199-1200, 3139-3141, `products.rs:266-269, 969-971`.
- **Callee bodies read instead of contracts.** Mutation summaries (062), returned predicates (057), the symbolic callee walk (065), the variant tag from returns (061), selector narrowing through callee exits (058, 060). Each is interprocedural inference inside a "checker".
- **Build-speed machinery shaping correctness.** Rayon, record and replay, and `OnceLock` caches filled by whichever caller asks first (058 `flow.rs:8495`; 062 2706); memo keys are pointer addresses (059).
- **Limits of the fact store leak to users.** "bind a borrow of the selected element before testing its case" (058 `flow.rs:11656-11667`).
- **Hardware in flow.** SGDT/LGDT ten-byte writes (062 `flow_mutation.rs:4158-4188, 4890-4932`), asm register intrinsics (059), atomic release events (057).
- **Panics as invariants** (057 664, 5992-6329; 059 `flow_exits.rs:1497`; 062 4105).
- **Maps and sets in dense-id code** (057 2188; 060 777, 5699; 061 6252; 062 1307; 063 `flow_operand_values.rs:336`).
- **Thin tests.** 15 unit tests across the group; joins, widening, caller authentication, cleanup, the callee walk and flow_calls/flow_exits have none.

## What a rebuilt compiler must still do

- When code writes storage, it must forget exactly the facts that write can change, including facts held under aliases and caller referents, while keeping facts about sibling fields and unchanged tags (R1-R7, R59).
- It must give every call a sound write footprint: nothing for pure builtins, the callee's proven effect when known, and otherwise the ceiling the signature permits through exclusive references, cleanup and retired loans (R8-R13).
- It must check a callee's `requires` at the call and give the caller the callee's `ensures`, including memberships on the result, on `&mut` referents and on handed-back borrows, but only while the described storage is unchanged (R14-R22, R27).
- It must support recursive postconditions as assume-and-reproduce, with termination checked separately (R18).
- It must turn guards, `and`/`or` short-circuits and match arms into facts on each path: Boolean truth, integer bounds, case selection or exclusion, and a variant's `requires` (R31-R40).
- It must refuse a payload read unless the case is proved, while letting contrary evidence discharge payload contracts (R37, R36).
- It must give each state the join of every arrival's facts. A public entry gets only its declared premises; caller facts enter only closed private bodies (R41-R46).
- When a value moves by `let`, assignment, constructor or cast, its facts must move with it. Routed and alias domains need real provenance, and casts erase memberships unless they preserve the carrier (R47-R53). In the new design this becomes "the fact stays on the value".
- It must classify every use as a move, an observation or a copy, from parameter types and multiplicity, the same way for statement and expression forms (R54-R58).
- It must track loans to their last use, join borrow debts by union and facts by intersection, and tie returned or stored references to exact argument storage and lifetime roots (R23-R26, R59-R64).
- It must plan cleanup for every dying owned value exactly and in order, and refuse linear values that have no cleanup machine (R65-R67).
- It must keep atomic ordering and byte-class rules for strings and byte stores (R13, R52).
