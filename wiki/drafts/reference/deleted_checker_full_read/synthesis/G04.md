`cc` = `checks_contracts.rs`, `calls` = `checks_contracts_calls.rs`, `exits` = `checks_contracts_exits.rs`, `prover` = `checks_contracts_prover.rs`, `writes` = `checks_contracts_writes.rs`, `csv` = `checks_current_scalar_values.rs`.

## What this code was

This is the contract checker of the old stage 05. For each call it decided whether the caller had established every `requires` of the callee (calls:3227-3567); for each normal exit, whether every `ensures` held (exits:10135-10412); for each write, whether a value entering a place declared `in D` was a member of `D` (writes:1580-1911); and for each return, whether the returned value met the domains its type declares (exits:3954-5053). One driver ran these in a fixed order (cc:11676-11935). None of the checks proved anything in one place: each ran an ordered list of "routes", every route a Rust function returning `bool` or `Option<bool>`, and accepted when any route said yes (calls:110-186; exits:10260-10347). The routes drew their premises from three sources: fact contexts published by the flow stage, values captured when call operands were evaluated (cc:6447-8141), and a per-statement table of current integers that `csv` rebuilt by replaying the state from entry (csv:4886-5228). Arithmetic was handed to `validation::scoped_arithmetic_implication`, which returned a three-valued judgment and no proof object (prover:1769-1770; csv:6000). The group also holds a loop-invariant inference engine (exits:556-3030) and a once-only accounting of performed hardware instructions (prover:6558-8150). The 44,483 lines contain three unit tests, none on a language rule (calls:4395-4564; exits:5742-5782; csv:959-1006).

## Mechanisms

**Route ladders.** `boolean_requirements::proves` tries eleven routes with short-circuit `||`, then splits `&&` (calls:99-207). `check_exit_ensures` does the same with about twelve (exits:10260-10347). No premise from one route reaches another (calls:12-13).

**Captured-operand binding.** For each observation a callee predicate makes on a formal, `call_values` finds the caller operand, fetches every value it could have had, rewrites the predicate onto literals or canonical places, and records one epoch per binding (cc:6447-7908). `capture_preserves_source` then decides whether the formal may be read as the caller's current storage (calls:1069-1266). No budget.

**Predicate transport over selections.** `captured_predicates` keeps one obligation per possible source of a copied value and accepts when the union of supporting selection conditions covers every possibility (cc:6039-6444).

**Denotation comparison.** Substitutes formals, expands immutable Boolean locals, peels borrows, then matches node kinds; 4096 steps, depth 64, exhaustion refuses (cc:1887-2054). A second structural matcher across two namespaces has depth 64 (calls:1714-1821); a third has a pending-pair list and no commutativity (prover:5198-5277).

**Closed evaluator.** `Option<bool>` and `Option<i64>` over literals, declared ranges, lengths and immutable initializers, with two cycle stacks (cc:3891-3957).

**Interval engines.** Seven separate interval comparers: cc:88-99, 3286-3355, 5198-5208, 5390-5404, 10455-10469; exits:8053-8089; writes:302-334. Bounds are `i64` in most (cc:5110-5208; csv:496-497).

**Arithmetic adapter.** Three adapters translate admitted expressions into `ScopedArithmeticExpression` and ask `validation`: at calls (cc:4419-4561), for call guarantees (prover:1363-2635), and from the current-values frame (csv:6743-6845, budget 4096, depth 64). Unknowns are atoms named by formatted strings.

**Header-invariant induction.** Candidates come from each exit's `ensures`; demands propagate backwards along state-simple paths; components are proved in SCC order; 16,384 work units per pass, substitution depth 64 (exits:1749-2003, 2020-2021). Discovery that runs out of budget returns what it has; proving that runs out refuses (exits:1863-1865, 1956-1958).

**Completion closure.** Worklist over (machine, state, subjects) through transitions and tail calls, with a visited set that treats recursion coinductively (exits:1266-1617).

**Linear forms.** Constant plus `i64` coefficients over atoms with cancellation, expanding locals and calls to depth 8, solely for `(a / d) * d` (exits:6942-7568).

**Guard refinement.** Narrows a range per guard conjunct, 4096 steps (exits:6784-6905).

**Prefix replay.** `CurrentScalarValues` rebuilds four tables (scalars, fields, lengths, saved conditions) from state entry on every query, retiring entries the storage graph does not preserve (csv:4705-5499). A memo for completed-call guarantees and re-anchoring exist to repair the cost (csv:213-322, 5384-5392).

**Guarantee admission with memo.** `scan` and `admit` decide once per (statement, ordinal, fact, expression) whether a callee `ensures` is usable (prover:3001-3358).

**Evaluation-order walk.** `ConstructionWalk` threads a set of fact contexts through an expression, intersecting at `match` arms and replacing the set at calls (writes:1962-2110).

**Instruction-effect receipts.** A search over context facts builds a receipt tree per requirement; `overlaps` refuses to spend one event twice (prover:7689-7974).

**Label renderer.** Turns expressions into text and substitutes formal names by argument text (cc:10750-11350; calls:1929-2052).

**Unbounded loops.** Element enumeration to a range's own end (prover:133-141), label recursion with no cycle guard (cc:11092-11105), operand provenance walk with no depth limit (cc:7910-8101).

## The three properties, tested

**1. Acceptance was the code's own yes or no.** For: every report searched its slice for `proof_admission`, `ProofNode`, `verify_obligation` and "certificate" and found no call in 44,483 lines. The word "certificate" appears only in comments that deny one (csv:6-8; writes:22-23; prover:2860). Evidence terms are "checked" by `==` on records built from labels (cc:995-996, 1289-1290). Against: four kinds of record are written, but none is rechecked: instruction-effect receipts (prover:7660-7687), `CheckedValueQualification` rows (writes:2293-2313), `CheckedScalarReturnQualification` with an origin tag (exits:4062-4073) and `CheckedEntryIndexBound` rows (csv:3898-3908). Deferred obligations (R69) postpone a numeric condition; they certify nothing. Verdict: holds without exception. No place in this group used the proof kernel.

## Patterns not seen before

**Comments that state a guarantee the code does not keep.** At least 18 across the group. Examples: "one interval engine" beside six more comparers (cc:41-55); "No later fallback may replace it" (calls:234-235 vs 3404-3412); "any call statement defeats the route" (calls:4091-4092 vs 4156-4184); "ambiguous origins grant no equality" (exits:2701-2702 vs 2732-2734); three "no initializer is replayed" comments beside a prover that replays initializers and callee bodies (exits:5792-5793, 6527-6528, 8132-8133 vs 7469-7507, 7306). The comments read as design rules written during a migration that the code had not finished.

**The same decision trusted in a circle.** `writes` accepts a call result's domain because the callee's exits check it (writes:340); `exits` accepts a tail call's declared return type for the same reason (exits:4016-4021); `calls` does too (calls:3830-3832, which names only scalar result domains). Entry values trust declared ranges as "store-enforced" (cc:3585-3591), and the store check for exact intervals is not in `writes` but in a range check outside this group (writes:14-15, 1826-1827). No slice closes the loop.

**"No diagnostic" or a recorded row read as proof.** A call's `requires` are "decided" when the diagnostic count did not change (cc:11804-11812); recursion is admitted when a diagnostic string is empty (prover:3415); qualification rows are read back as proof (writes:376-386; exits:4554-4563); postconditions come from validation's list, "nothing rejudges it" (cc:9059-9062); an `ensures` in `entailment.expressions`, or one carrying an evidence term, is skipped at the exit (exits:10184-10209). No report in this group found where a witness-bearing `ensures` is discharged.

**Twins that drift.** Six pairs answer one question differently: the four write copies (033), statement versus expression calls (cc:5066), two definitions of the exit's returned value (cc:8643 vs 11577), three behaviours on ambiguous origins (exits:810, 1057, 2732), entry-state versus current-state return types (exits:4125 vs 4418), receiver offset (three sites).

**A callback that is always the constant true.** `evaluate_scalar`/`evaluate_for_atoms` receive `|_, _| true` at ten sites in four files (cc:2907, 2910, 3424; calls:344, 617; exits:3364, 3772, 9403, 9538, 9892). No reader found a caller that passes a real filter.

**Silent returns on a failed lookup.** Roughly 35 sites return without a diagnostic when a machine, state, call site or definition is missing (for example cc:244-261, 694-696, 1569-1587; calls:4582-4697; exits:3962-3968, 5265-5335; writes:1590-1594). Each means "not checked".

**Whether a context list is a conjunction or a set of alternatives is never stated.** Five provers accept on any one context. `writes` intersects context sets across `match` arms (writes:1975-1990), which fits "jointly live bundles"; `call_values` says contexts from different selections "are never a joint premise set" (cc:7051-7054) and `calls` requires every case to prove (calls:3421-3427). Two readers could not tell which holds.

**Budgets chosen per function.** 4096/64 (cc:1889-1900; csv:6760; exits:6631), 16,384 (exits:2020), 256 (calls:724), 128 (exits:3552), 8 (calls:2751; exits:6940; csv:5901), and none at all in four walks. Exhaustion refuses, except invariant discovery, which silently keeps a partial result.

**Performance repairs layered on a replay design.** Three memos carry comments naming the quadratic cost they repair (csv:213-218; prover:3076-3091, 5483-5486); `csv` rebuilds a whole frame to return one number (csv:6879-6895).

**Debug tracing left in the proof path** in six of eight slices (cc:1630-1728, 11769-11783; calls:818-824; exits:691-965, 8018-8023; prover:1035-1278).

**A value-and-epoch core already existed.** `captured_predicates`, `call_values`, capture preservation, storage-graph anchors and the guarantee admission checks track values by observation row and read epoch (cc:5426-6445; calls:990-1355; csv:5394-5499; prover:3290-3358). The text-matching routes sit beside them as older fallbacks.

## What a rebuilt compiler must still do

- Prove every `requires` at every call and every `ensures` at every normal exit, per conjunct, from one set of facts (R1, R33, R55).
- Judge a call's requirement on the values its operands had when evaluated, and refuse premises that an operand evaluated in between may have written (R2, R3).
- Use the selected arm's guard, the negated guards of earlier arms and case tests as premises, and discharge any obligation on an arm whose guard is refuted (R4, R5, R64).
- Bind named evidence arguments to the callee's `requires` propositions by identity of the proposition, and check conformance arguments close (R6, R7, R8).
- Require domain membership for every value entering a domained place, in every placement, including arguments, returns, transfers and referents of exclusive references on return and after a loan (R11, R12, R26-R31).
- Keep routed domains apart from predicate-only ones: only an authorized route or an existing membership establishes a routed domain; a test or cast cannot mint it (R13, R21, R22).
- Prove predicate-only membership from all predicates and parents, match indexed domains by instance, and apply the preservation laws for windows, concatenation, string literals and implied domains (R14-R18).
- Decide when a declared type may be trusted for membership (call results, immutable parameters) and when only a live fact counts (R19, R20, R67).
- Drop a membership when an expression erases it and condition payload facts on the selected case (R23, R24, R25).
- Admit a completed call's `ensures` as a caller fact only under ownership, stability and no-overwrite conditions, and a recursive call's only after strict rank descent (R50, R51, R52).
- Infer or accept invariants for looping state machines and follow guarantees through tail calls (R36, R37).
- Reason about `result`: its case, equality with a returned parameter, integer bounds, lengths, and inherited contracts (R34, R35, R38-R42).
- Give arithmetic meaning only to builtin operators, respect each arithmetic domain and cast kind, and treat lengths as descriptor observations (R53, R54, R56, R62).
- Join facts at `match`, keep known array elements through windows and unknown indices, and check index bounds of entry projections (R63, R65, R66).
- Invalidate facts on writes by exact storage, including through aliases, calls and assembly (R10, R41, R43, R44, R61).
- Prove transparent propositions, structural requirements, float predicate domains and embeddings (R45-R48).
- Account for performed instruction effects once each, and check carry permissions and origins (R58, R59).
- Refuse an activation claim that escapes by return, a refuted boundary obligation, and a concatenation stored into a borrowed view (R32, R49, R68).
- Defer target-dependent numeric conditions as explicit obligations and settle them later (R69).
- Validate static const-generic applications before reading their values (R57).
