# Group G10a: validation: access views and write-only borrows, integer and float ranges, borrow contracts and reflection, call cycles and termination ranking

File names are shortened in citations: `avb.rs` = `access_views_and_borrows.rs` (slice 085), `ad.rs` = `arithmetic_domains.rs` (slices 086, 087, 088), `bar.rs` = `borrow_and_reflection.rs` (slice 089), `cc.rs` = `call_cycles.rs` (slice 090). All four lived in `omega-rust/psi/compilation/validation/src/`.

## What this code was

These six slices are about 40,000 lines of the old checker's validation crate. `avb.rs` checked compiler-generated "access views" (accessor records over placed or atomic storage) and enforced the `&write` rule that a write-only reference may be replaced or forwarded but never read (avb.rs:1-4, 3720-3785). `ad.rs`, 27,000 lines in three slices, was the numeric reasoner: an expression walker that gives every integer or float expression an interval and refuses unproven `Exact` overflow, zero divisors, bad shift counts, mixed carriers and uncovered `Trapping` operations (ad.rs:5581-8889); a statement walk and per-machine fixed point that carry facts between states (ad.rs:9895-12484); and a "value environment" of facts with rules for dropping them after writes, plus an ordering theory over operands (ad.rs:18144-22227, 25897-26918). `bar.rs` computed which inputs a returned view borrows from, checked that one callable refines another's borrow contract, and replayed reflection products against the program (bar.rs:7-3734, 3736-5587). `cc.rs` built the machine call graph and decided whether each recursive group was allowed: contract cycles never, proof-only cycles with a structural or interval ranking, runtime cycles only as tail calls with a non-increasing measure (cc.rs:3719-3792, 249-567, 2545-3142). None of the six slices calls the proof kernel. Most functions are oracles that return an interval or a boolean for a caller elsewhere; `ad.rs` 8892-18142 creates only three diagnostics itself (ad.rs:15104-15337).

## Mechanisms

- **Expression walk (abstract interpreter).** `ExpressionWalk` returns `{recipe, domain, interval, primitive}` for any expression and accumulates "decisions"; five entry points set three flags (ad.rs:8448-8494, 8539-8888). It serves R26 to R45.
- **Statement walk and per-machine fixed point.** `ArrivalWalk` carries a `ValueEnvironment` through a state's statements (ad.rs:11086-12272). `incoming_environments` starts every state with no facts and repeats until stable or `states.len() + 1` rounds (ad.rs:10846-11014). It serves every "bounds at this occurrence" query.
- **Value environment.** Text-keyed maps and sets plus a relation list; rebind by string prefix, invalidate by string overlap, join by intersection (ad.rs:26012-26030, 26218-26489, 26809-26916).
- **Ordering theories, three of them.** A premise-table prover with budget `premises² + 4096` (ad.rs:15639-15986). A difference graph with union-find and longest-gap relaxation, at most `n - 1` rounds (ad.rs:19261-19547). A congruence closure limited to 256 terms, 65,536 comparisons and depth 128 (ad.rs:20241-20426, 19877-19878). A fourth, `DifferenceBounds`, runs Floyd–Warshall per component with no size budget (ad.rs:1422-1587).
- **Refutation through the arithmetic engine.** `contradicts` turns relations, congruences, carrier bounds and stored intervals into comparisons over atoms named `observation:{n}` and asks `scoped_arithmetic_comparisons_unsatisfiable` (ad.rs:18541-18646, 18704).
- **Interval types, four of them.** `Option<i64>` ends (ad.rs:16353-16601), `i128` range sets that can hold a hole (ad.rs:16604-16734), `BigInt` intervals (ad.rs:903-1289, 16777-16821), and symbolic `IntegerRangeRecipe`s that defer a bound to a later stage (ad.rs:2399-2465, 6493-6513).
- **Contract projection.** A callee's `requires` and `ensures` are read into a fresh environment keyed by parameter name to bound the call result (ad.rs:1807-2313).
- **Return-range inference.** The caller's check walks the callee's body, follows transitions into further machines, and returns nothing on a cycle (ad.rs:9919-10327).
- **Fact transport on copy.** `CopiedIntegerValue` copies intervals, exact values, float facts and recipes from source to destination, keyed by both a structural path and a text suffix (ad.rs:3823-4565).
- **Trap coverage by complement.** The environment is narrowed by the negation of every `crashes Trap` guard and the operation is checked again; entry state only (ad.rs:8169-8257).
- **Layout reach.** For every layout target, decides whether a storage coordinate holds a laid-out integer whose write is not total (ad.rs:23690-23863).
- **Write-only projection resolution and root tainting.** A climb over member and index steps with budget `usize::MAX`, tracking referee type and access (avb.rs:1789-1898); `expression_mentions_root` walks a whole expression (avb.rs:3849-3927).
- **Erased-descendant search.** Depth 64, 256 records, memo list; any exhausted budget answers "contains erased" and so refuses (avb.rs:504-612).
- **Carried-lifetime walk.** One recursive walk over a type in six modes produces the list of borrows it carries; fixed arrays are unrolled per element with no cap (bar.rs:1378-2170, 1788-1804).
- **Callable region inference.** A unification of fresh regions that must end in exactly one application (bar.rs:2964-3327).
- **Replay by recomputation.** Rebuild a product from the program, compare whole, then compare an FNV-style hash (bar.rs:3753-4037, 5044-5128, 5485-5528; avb.rs:914-1163).

## What a rebuilt compiler must still do

- Refuse any read of a `&write` referent, at every depth, while allowing replacement, exact forwarding and `.len` of its own descriptor (R16, R17, R24, R25).
- Limit `&write` to referee types whose old contents may be discarded, and keep the referee type exact when forming or re-lending (R15, R18, R19, R22, R23).
- Prove index and range bounds for stores through `&write` (R20; the old code did not).
- Refuse `&write` across a boundary that has no checked body (R14, R21).
- Check a custody record against its schema: erased fields present with equal type and multiplicity, represented fields absent, nothing extra (R1 to R5).
- Enforce atomic accessor permissions and package privacy of accessors (R9 to R13).
- Require one carrier and one arithmetic domain per operator and per store, with explicit `as` to change either (R26, R27, R28).
- Prove `Exact` arithmetic, narrowing stores, returns and casts representable (R29, R30, R31, R32, R40, R41, R42).
- Prove divisors nonzero and `MIN / -1` excluded, and shift counts within width (R33, R34, R35).
- Land untyped numeric values exactly, including in `addr` (R38, R39), and restrict address arithmetic (R37).
- Require each `Trapping` operation to be covered by a `crashes Trap` route or proven safe, and keep `Trapping` out of specifications (R43, R44).
- Keep specification arithmetic total, with premises ordered clause by clause (R33, R48, R53, R74).
- Prove a call's `requires` at the call site and use its `ensures` afterwards, including the union over a target family (R47, R59, R60).
- Prove that a value stored into a data value honours that type's `requires`, and that a laid-out integer fits its stored width on every target (R49, R50).
- Derive facts from guards, `match` arms, variant tests, boolean locals, declared ranges and domains (R54, R55, R62, R63, R64, R65), carry them through copies (R66), and drop them on any write that may reach the value, including cleanup hooks (R56, R57, R58).
- Decide orderings between integer terms, including collection lengths and subslices (R52, R68, R69, R70).
- Reason about float ranges and not-NaN (R73).
- Compute which inputs a returned view borrows from, refuse ambiguity, and enforce access compatibility (R78, R79, R80, R81, R82).
- Check that a callable value refines a required callable's regions and returned-view sources (R75, R76, R77), and that owned carriers keep their loans (R83).
- Validate reflection requests: visibility, unique resolution of every named declaration, selection coverage, no visit of erased members (R86 to R90).
- Forbid contract-to-contract call cycles (R91).
- Require a ranking for proof-only recursion, structural or by a proven strict decrease (R94, R95, R103), and keep proof-only and runtime machines out of one cycle (R93).
- Require tail calls in runtime recursion and, where termination is promised, a non-increasing measure with a strict decrease on every cycle, for each of the seven measure forms (R92, R97 to R105).
