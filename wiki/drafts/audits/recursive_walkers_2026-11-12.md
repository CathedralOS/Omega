## Shared tree types (one flat table retires several walkers)

- **SyntaxTrees (stage 01):** `expressions`, `statements`, `patterns`, and
  `types` arena tables — all parse/lowering walkers in stage 01 share these
  handles; one indexed edge list per syntactic sort retires the mutual
  `parse_*_handle` SCC.
- **SymbolResolvedTrees (stage 02):** resolution/lowering walks the same
  syntax-backed tables after symbol stamping.
- **TypedTrees (stages 03–04):** expression/type lowering and elaboration
  share `TypedTrees` expression/statement tables.
- **CheckedTrees + validation (stage 05):** flow, borrow, contract, and
  validation visitors share `TypedTrees`/`CheckedTrees` operand graphs —
  largest cluster count.
- **Lowered Psi (stage 06):** emission/lowering walks checked units and
  expression preparation tables once lowered.
