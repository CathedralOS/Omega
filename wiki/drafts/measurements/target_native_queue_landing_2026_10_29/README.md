## Fresh integration result

Retain the application-owned queue for publication. The heavy four-target
stack32 compile fell from **2682.9 to 1749.2 ms (34.8%)**, with all four
balanced block reductions positive: 37.8%, 27.5%, 27.3% and 34.7%.
Each producer has eight timed observations. Baseline range: 2348.2–2977.8 ms;
candidate: 1660.2–2198.9 ms. These are fresh matched complete-process timings
on the integrated source, not the historical 41% observation relabeled.

| Workload | Four-target median change | Single-target median change |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| length | 6.1% lower | 0.2% lower |
| dispatch | 2.5% higher | 1.5% higher |
| stack32 | 34.8% lower | 3.1% higher |

Small and single-target results do not establish a general speedup. All valid
observations are retained, with zero exclusions in the complete cohort. The
failed initial cohort remains separate. Profile captures and first visits are
not mixed into timed medians, and no repeated cold benefit is claimed.

Confirmed heavy-case median root CPU rose 2664.1→2796.9 ms (5.0%) and peak job
private commit rose 399.36→506.71 MiB (+107.35 MiB, 26.9%). The gain is latency
through overlap, with a resource cost. The cap is per roster, not process-wide;
the 256 MiB worker stack is reserved address space, not that much actual commit.

The complete comparison checked 294 pinned images across 114 seed/timed/profile
compiles and observed 114 actual Windows exits 0. All nine native/Terminal
agreements and authored reject controls, six malformed-record recoveries,
five source/root/pruning controls, partial-target admission/order/duplicate
control, and four-job native scalar success/refusal controls passed again.
Representative ELF, Mach-O and PE machine headers were independently checked.
Foreign images were not executed. No full corpus or foreign-host qualification
is inferred from these checks or the small merge-queue admission check.
