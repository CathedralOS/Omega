What is real

      
Real: the source, its tokens, the states and edges, and the run's values. Sum 131 and count 2 give the result 133, matching the sample's recorded input grid.

      
Illustration: every compiler stage after the tokens, and the check sequence. They show the kind of work each stage does, not the compiler's output. The order follows the pipeline: Psi, Terminal Psi, then Omega. Nothing here says the program terminates; it is not a native run.
