## Scope summary

This file implements the Selected Lowering literal-fold rewrite pass: pair rules fold a lone `MaterializeI64` producer into a sole-use consumer (U12 immediates, extensions, copies, identity folds, fault/obligation discharge folds) and rewrite the selected-instruction plan (`read`, `literal_fold_rewrites.rs:1-6`, `1214-1267`, `2365-2404`, `4064-4104`). Sparse conditional constant propagation and value-range analysis are not implemented here (`read`, lines 1–3000 searched; no such modules in range).

Compile-time scalar work in range is concentrated in `SelectedInstructionPairRule::fold_immediate`, extension masks/sign-extends in that function, `PairImmediateBound::admits_immediate`, `rewrite_consumer` embedding/`u32::try_from`, and `scalar_materialize_value` (`read`, `3841-3867`, `3833-3838`, `3898-3990`, `3993-4003` — extra lines).
