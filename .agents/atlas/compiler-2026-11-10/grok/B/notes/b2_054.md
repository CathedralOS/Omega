## When a fold is refused

Per candidate, the pass skips with `continue` or `None`: non-`MaterializeI64` producer, `u64::try_from` failure, register/sole-use checks, `admit_candidate` failure, zero or multiple matching rules (`read`, `1224-1262`, `1437-1550`, `1603-1611`).

Whole-pass refusal returns `LiteralFoldError`, including `WorkOverflow` and `BudgetExceeded` from `checked_*` on host counters (`read`, `2220-2343`, `1751-1782`). Staging stops the loop when `applied_count == 0` (`read`, `359-360`).
