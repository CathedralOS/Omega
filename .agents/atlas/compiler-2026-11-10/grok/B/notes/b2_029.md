5. If adjacent pair shares same `(machine, owner, obligation)` → `Err(DuplicateOwner)` (`proof.rs:533-538`).
6. Assign `unit.accepted_obligation_facts = facts` (`proof.rs:539`).
7. `unit.identity = super::recompute_psi_optimization_unit_identity(&unit)` (`proof.rs:540-541`).
8. Return `Ok(unit)` (`proof.rs:541`).

**Lookup after attach (`read`):** `PsiOptimizationUnit::accepted_obligation_fact(machine, owner, obligation)` finds first matching fact in `accepted_obligation_facts` (`proof.rs:127-136`).
