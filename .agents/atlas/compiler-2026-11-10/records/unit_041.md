## 1. 01_tokens-to-syntax-trees/src/type_syntax_and_patterns.rs

**Purpose.** Parser module for (a) structural destructure patterns `{ field, field as name, field: expr, .. ; proof selectors }` used in match arms, with a rewriter that substitutes destructured names inside guard expressions, and (b) type reference grammar (`&`, `()`, `[T]`, `[T; N]`, `dyn Trait<..>`, `Name<args>`, `T in Domain<..> & ..`), `[copy|linear|carry(..)]` data properties, and qualified static names. **Shape (read).**
