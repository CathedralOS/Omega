## Five things a maintainer most needs to know about this unit
1. `captured_place_compatibility` (overlap.rs:2924) is the one spatial judgment under almost every check in this unit (reads, moves, call arguments, aggregates, incoming backing). Two reads are always non-interfering (3003-3013); a pair where either side carries an `incoming_reference` and the families differ is treated as independent (2989-2992); different symbol roots are disjoint, so correctness of aliasing depends on callers resolving backings first (`place_access`, `access_sources`, `aliases`).
2. `check_flow_call_borrows` (checks_borrows.rs:11381) is the single entry (1 call site, checks.rs:12059) and runs state checks in a rayon pool; a flow state with no matching `StateBorrowFact` is skipped (11434-11435) in both it and `calls::check_compatibility` (calls.rs:1497).
3. Loan authority is carried as "lineage" handles and "descriptor occurrences" (`aliases::same_descriptor` 8195, `descriptor_lineage` 8005); several modules (`access_sources`, `completed_backings`, `formation_captures`, `place_access`, `staged_values`) all resolve "what storage does this reference value denote" by walking flow-operand graphs, each with its own DFS and `Err(&'static str)` reason strings. Failures give `None`/`Err`, which become "without an exact retained backing" diagnostics or are skipped (see section 9 sites).
