## integer_predicates tail (54637-55143)

How it decides: keyed on term shape. `project_integer_comparison` peels up to 64 boolean-equality wrappers to find a polarity (54696-54705), then takes either a `MathematicalBinary` comparison (54706) or a `ScalarBinary` comparison whose operand primitives accept integer literals (54747-54751). `mathematical_value` handles only Integer, CollectionMeasure (forced to `U64`, 54997-54998), the `AddressBound` builtin, IntegerEmbedding and Add/Subtract/Multiply (54995-55029; any other operator returns `None`, 55026).
