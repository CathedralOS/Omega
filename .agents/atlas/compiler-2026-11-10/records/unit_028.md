## Five things a maintainer most needs to know about this unit
1. These three crates are data plus identity hashing plus wire codecs; none makes a compile decision. Each decoder checks magic, exact version, closed tags, trailing bytes and a recomputed SHA-256 identity, and nothing semantic (fragments/publication.rs:5-6, PAM lib.rs:24-26, RH lib.rs:31-37). A re-authenticated artifact with wrong contents decodes fine (PAM codec/tests.rs:846-855).
2. Wire versions and identity-domain versions are independent numbers that do not move together: PAM wire 27 vs identity domain v26 (codec.rs:36, identity.rs:28); FCV wire 98 vs envelope domain `v58` (fixed_view_copy_codec.rs:55, :57); RH register-home wire 7 vs domain v7; manifests OMGFFE v15 / identity v15, OMGTSP v16 / v16, OMGPAO v16 / v7. Retired versions are enumerated in tests (e.g. recovery/codec_tests.rs:586-603 rejects 0..32).
3. Some fields are not covered by the identity hash or are dropped on the wire: layout identity omits `normalized_foreign_call_fixup` (layout.rs:326-332); text-section identity omits `callback_materializations` (text_section.rs:282-308); the fixed-view-copy codec drops `qualification_establishments`, target-parameter `projected_qualifications` and the settlement `realization` (structural_calls.rs:1476, structural_declarations.rs:518, structural_contracts.rs:404-405).
