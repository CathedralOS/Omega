## Five things a maintainer most needs to know about this unit

1. Identity bytes are the contract and they are written by several separate encoders: three path-segment tables with different tag numbers for the same enum (LO/identity/structural_types.rs:187-225, SI/effects/program/identity.rs:455-492, SI/plan_identity.rs:587-622), plus duplicate work/scalar-type/cleanup/ownership/crash-guard encoders. Changing one does not change the others.
2. `legalized_operation_plan_identity` omits two things I confirmed by reading: the runtime-index rows on structural arguments of ordinary calls (calling.rs:221-227, structural_types.rs:123-172, target-operations values.rs:38) and `StructuralTypeDeclaration.quotient_carrier` (structural_types.rs:285-394). No test mutates either.
3. The instruction tag table in LO/identity/scalar_graph.rs is not collision-free despite its header comment: saturating tags (43+, 51+, 59+, 73+, 87+ plus special 35/36/40/41/42) overlap literal tags 40-46 and 88-94 of other instruction shapes, and tag 92 is used by two non-arithmetic arms (scalar_graph.rs:568, 830). Payloads differ, so I did not find an actual ambiguous pair.
4. Selected-instruction plan identity is incremental and memoised: `SelectedFunctions` pages (64 entries), per-function register pages (64 registers), per-block instruction chunks (64/32). Reuse is keyed on `Arc` pointer equality and on `prefix_verified`, which `SelectedFunctions::mutate_preserving_register_prefix` sets from the caller's promise (functions.rs:201-231, plan_identity.rs:206-321); only a `debug_assert!` checks the register-count part.
5. Machine-effect wire format (`OMGMFX`, version 49) decode is permissive in structure checks (any self-consistent identity decodes) and has constant-true version gates (`true, true, true` at encoding.rs:190, 773) and an unreachable `CatalogFenceV1` arm (encoding.rs:1037) because tag 19 is shared with `ReadTableFieldV1`.
