The arena ID, primitive type and staging-path fixtures reproduce failures on `1ab250720c8c`. Later repairs identify their own regressions. Every finding in sections 5 and 6 of [area 01](../digests/01-cli-foundation.md) has an individual disposition below. The specified source rewrite engine remains a release feature.
