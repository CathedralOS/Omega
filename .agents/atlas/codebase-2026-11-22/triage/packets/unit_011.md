  `control_flow.rs`, `control_flow_calls.rs`, `control_flow_operations.rs`,
  `control_flow_storage.rs` and `scalar.rs` read `reference_projection_regions`
  directly. Each needs the same check that it walks the returned `path`.

  `EstablishCase`, an `AccessLifecycle` operand or a `BoundarySettlement`

  rather than miscompile it. No fixture demonstrates the refusal yet.
