## Still open

- **Record EP `body_cpu.rs` §9; digest 10:196, 10:215: unlisted `MachineAlternativeFamily` values add no named state.** For x86-64 the unlisted state-touching families are `SystemRegisterRead/Write` (RFLAGS only), `InterruptControl`, `PortRegisterRead/Write`, `ProcessorHalt` and the fences. `footprint.decoded` records their declared register units: RFLAGS reads and writes declare the flags and `rsp`, and the port and RFLAGS forms declare their scratch registers. `check_mask_operation` refuses enabling interrupts in a masked handler. On AArch64 `SystemRegisterWrite` declares only scratch registers (`targets/target-aarch64/src/form_effects/register_access.rs`), and `body_cpu` records no `ControlState` for it. I did not establish which AArch64 system registers that family can write, or whether an AArch64 entry that saves state from this footprint is reachable. Next check: an AArch64 callable entry whose body writes a system register; compare the planned `BodyCpuEffects` with the state its bridge must preserve.
- **Duplicated mechanisms (record §6 items; digest 10:72-76).** These are code-quality leads with no correctness effect found: the ten-variant transformation list repeated in `rewrites.rs` macros and in the stage 07 tag numbering; `SameHomeComponents` and `tied_components`; `FailedDomain::in_function` and `in_function_indexed`; the vector and mask view-preference paths in `home_assignment.rs`; `next_instruction_id`/`next_register_id` in three places; settlement index shifting in three places; three hand-written root-agreement checks; two immediate-u64 rematerialization admissions. Each needs its own refactor with an equality test between the two paths.
- **Hard-coded shape limits (record "five things" item 4).** The 128-view bitmask path and the `u16` packed keys fall back to general paths. `physical_matrix` is quadratic in the view count. Performance leads only.
