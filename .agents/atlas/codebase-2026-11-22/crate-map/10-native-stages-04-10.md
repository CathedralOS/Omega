## 1. What the area does

These are the native-compiler stages that turn one function body from target-neutral operations into a body with a physical frame, ready to be encoded. Stage 04 lowers optimized abstract operations to target operations (calling conventions, byte layouts, boundary settlements) and prepares an execution plan. Stage 05 selects machine instructions with virtual registers. Stage 06 rewrites those instructions before allocation, and the `analyses` crate supplies liveness, live ranges and register legality to stages 06 and 07. Stage 07 assigns physical register homes and recovers from register pressure by spilling, splitting and rematerializing. Stage 08 picks one physical instruction form for every instruction and runs the post-allocation rules. Stage 09 plans the frame (callee-save slots, stack layout, save/restore recipes), the call plans and the stack demand. Its sibling crate `entry-planning` plans entry, adapter and import bodies. Stage 10 binds the installation authority (interrupt handlers, stack guards) to the framed body. The input is `optimized::OptimizedAbstractBody` from stage 03 (outside this area). The output is `BoundFunctionBody`, which stage 11 (`native-encoding`) encodes. The driver `driver/compile_body.rs` calls these stages in order (read, `compile_body.rs:38-160`).
