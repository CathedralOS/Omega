## Five things a maintainer most needs to know about this unit

1. Refusal is the default exit. Nearly every missing row becomes `refuse(...)` (reason `Unsupported`) or `internal(...)` (also `Unsupported`, detail prefix "completion"); only `reject` produces `Violation` (`lib.rs:308,:322`, `lower.rs:1254-1264`). A call cycle lowers as a loop only if every in-cycle call is terminal, has no selected public contract, the machines have no erased/rigid values, and any authored crash routes are unguarded (`components.rs:153-201`); a `Promised` termination needs every reachable cyclic member to have a ranking (`:153`).
