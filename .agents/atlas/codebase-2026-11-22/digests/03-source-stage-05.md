## 1. What this area does

Stage 05 is one crate, `prepared-typed-trees-to-lowered-program` (114 files, 97,047 lines). It takes the `PreparedTypedTrees` stage 04 froze and a `Compilation` naming the roots, reads every reachable machine into a closed body, works out each machine's outcomes and call cycles, lowers each body into a `TerminalModule` with its `MachineTables`/`CheckTables`, and binds the result to its source correspondence as a `LoweredSourcePublication` (`compilation.rs:21`, `:45`, `:56`). It proves nothing: it mints proof obligations and leaves them to stage 06 (`lower.rs:15`, "Obligations are created, never discharged"). The driver calls it at `driver/judge_lowered_source.rs:114` (`lower_for_publication`) and `:116` (`source_roster::lower_roster`); both go through `prepare_compilation` (`compilation.rs:83`). Most failures leave as refusals, and three `Violation` sites are named in `unit_059`: "value machine can fall off its end" (`lower.rs:1115`), "use the returned value or explicitly discard it" (`lower/control.rs:401`), whole-value replacement of a linear value (`lib.rs:2104-2115`); `read/rejections.rs:91-217` is the reader's single place that turns a type mismatch into a `Violation`.

**Formation check (377 lines).** `check_bound_callable_shapes` (`callable_formations.rs:21`) runs first and compares each bound root slot's required calling shape with the machine it names, by symbol and `normalized_type_identity` (`:374`). A mismatch returns `CallableFormationFailure` rows and `prepare_compilation` stops (`compilation.rs:89-103`).

**Reader (about 55,900 lines in `read.rs` and `read/`, 58 files).** In: `TypedTrees`. Out: `IdTable<MachineId, Body>` and `Signature` rows in the `body.rs` vocabulary (1,471 lines; `ValueType`, `Statement`, `ExpressionKind`, `Signature`). State is one `Program` (`read.rs:156`, about 80 fields). Work happens in `read/machine.rs` (`machine` `:461`, admission gate `machine_shape` `:1518`, body driver `read_body` `:4205`), with family modules for calls (`read/calls.rs`, 4,577 lines), contracts, expressions, places, types, services and so on. `read_bodies` (`lib.rs:455`) pulls in each callee a body discovers. `read/services.rs:186` (`finish_operational_envelopes`) computes each callable's operational envelope to a fixed point.

**Whole-compilation preparation (about 19,200 lines, 32 files).** `elaborate_source_for` (`lib.rs:369`) reads entries, then `finish_reading_into` (`lib.rs:1102`) loops reading bodies, binder reach, calling policies and envelopes to a fixed point (`:1119-1155`), then runs `lower_program` (`lib.rs:1517`) and publishes reach, layouts, declarations, provider candidates, access plans, nominal calls, readiness, reference results, storage plan, opaque carriers and crossings (`:1156-1412`). Around it: `outcome::outcomes` (`outcome.rs:106`, callee components first), `components.rs:130` (call cycles admitted as loops), `declare.rs:163` (declaration identities and contract commitments; `close_specializations` `:1519`), `qualifications.rs`, `reach.rs:386`, `proof_scope.rs:73`, `calling_publication.rs:261`, `provider_selections.rs:33`, `reference_results.rs:27`, `reference_ends.rs:46`, `suspension.rs:23`, `selection_questions.rs:1220` (`capture_with_bodies`), and `publication.rs:104` (`finalize_source`, fixed order: proof scope, calling, providers, `:102-103`). `prepare_generic_forms` (`lib.rs:631`) and `prepare_witness` (`:791`) produce generic check forms and conformance witnesses.

**Lowering (about 11,800 lines, 8 files).** In: one `Body`, its `Signature`s and `Outcome`s. Out: a `TerminalMachine` plus table rows pushed in lockstep with `MachineSources` so ids agree (`lower.rs:3-7`). Per-machine entry is `lower::machine` (`lower.rs:130`), called from `lower_generic_for` (`lib.rs:1472`). `lower/places.rs` (5,669 lines) holds structural types, roots, projections, the write log, construction, copies and the sum-dispatch terminator; `lower/control.rs` blocks, edges, matches and crashes; `lower/calls.rs` calls, invocation rows and loans; `lower/operations.rs` scalar operations and `OperationSafety` obligations; `lower/ranking.rs` loop measures.

**Tests (about 8,700 lines in 15 test files, plus in-file test modules).** 209 `#[test]` under `src/` and 35 under `<05>/tests/` (checked in §10). `src/tests.rs` builds closed bodies by hand and skips the reader (`tests.rs:1`); `<05>/tests/*.rs` run real source through stages 00-04, edit one fact in the stage 04 product, and require lowering to follow it (`unit_063` item 1). The integration targets are declared in `source-to-psi/Cargo.toml:142-182`, not in this crate's manifest (`autotests = false`).
