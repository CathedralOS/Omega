# Digest: 11-native-stages-11-15

Paths are relative to `source-rust/subsystems/compiler/psi-to-native/pipeline/`. Short roots:
`S11/` = `11_bound-framed-machine-to-selected-form-encoding/src/`; `A64/` and `X64/` = that stage's `isa-aarch64/src/` and `isa-x86_64/src/`; `A64sfe` and `X64sfe` = the `selected_form_encoding.rs` file in each; `S12/` = `12_selected-form-encoding-to-resolved-layout/src/`; `S13/` = `13_resolved-layout-to-placed-functions/src/`; `S14/` = `14_placed-functions-to-objects/src/`; under `15_objects-to-image/`: `IMG/` = `image/src/`, `ELF/` = `image-elf/src/`, `MACHO/` = `image-macho/src/`, `PE/` = `image-pe/src/`, `EMI/` = `image-emission/src/`, `FF` = `EMI/function_fragments.rs`, `NA/` = `native-artifact/src/`, `DER/` = `NA/physical/derivation/`.

Words used below. A **selected form** is one machine instruction shape chosen by stage 08 (a `SelectedInstructionKind` plus an alternative key). A **footprint** is the registers, flags and encoded effects an encoder says its bytes touch. A **placeholder** is a zero call or relocation field left for a later stage (x86 `e8 00 00 00 00`, AArch64 `0x94000000`). A **replay** or **validator** recomputes a value from inputs and compares it with what a producer stored. The **audit** path runs only when the compile retained earlier stage inputs (`audit_requested`, `retain_original`). **Terminal Psi** is the internal last portable form; in `native-artifact` the records call one surviving operator or boundary occurrence tied to byte spans a **physical child**, and a derivation that cannot place a shape records a **gap** instead of refusing.

## 1. What this area does

Native stages 11 to 15 turn one function body, already allocated and framed by stage 10, into machine bytes and then into a finished file. Stage 11 encodes every selected instruction and the frame prologue and epilogue with an x86-64 or AArch64 encoder; stage 12 gives each encoded row an offset and shrinks x86 conditional branches; stage 13 builds the whole-function exit contract, inserts frame bytes, widens branches that fell out of reach and places all functions in one text section; stage 14 wraps the text in a relocation-free object (`OMGTRO`) and the `ObjectPlan`/`RelocationPlan` that image writers read; stage 15 composes entry bridges, imports and private objects, builds a format-neutral `FinalImage`, writes it as ELF, Mach-O or PE, and seals a `NativeArtifact` with physical evidence and publication receipts. The area is 13 crates and 153,323 lines (`crates.tsv`, sum checked against `units.tsv`). The driver calls stages 11 to 14 per body at `driver/compile_body.rs:193`, `:219`, `:246`, `:268` and `:271`, and stage 15 from `driver/image/object_emission.rs:260` and `driver/mod.rs:149` (crate map §3). Most of the line count is checking code and tests: roughly two thirds of `A64sfe` lines 1-14400 are tests (`unit_017`), about 2,650 of the last 2,917 lines of `X64sfe` are tests (`unit_018_gap_006`), and most of `FF` re-checks the object against earlier forms (`unit_022` item 2).
