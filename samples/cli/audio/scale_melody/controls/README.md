# Scale playback compiler controls

Owning outcome: **BUILD-OWNED-NATIVE-BINDINGS**, preserving the original
`scale_melody` remainder, WAV and playback journey.

[private_period](private_period/main.omg) keeps the guarded stored divisor
through a private call and forms the original wrapping remainder. Inputs31
and3 execute the operation; input0 exercises the guard-false return. Its
[zero-arrival twin](private_period/invalid/main.omg) changes only `>0` to
`>=0`. Separate controls retain refusal after an RHS mutation, on a public
arbitrary-input helper, under a later guard, and for an independently partial
child. No result comparison establishes operation formation.

[exact_period_pair](exact_period_pair/main.omg) additionally excludes the
simultaneous signed MIN/-1 pair; its twin admits that actual pair and must
reject even though its mathematical remainder is zero. Both borrowed operands
are captured before the operation, and every private arrival must justify the
formation conditions. Missing/unknown arrivals contribute no proof.

[composed_refinement](composed_refinement/main.omg) carries a qualified local
inside a recursive composed helper. Its invariant belongs to the actual emitted
Terminal machine and completed SSA value, rather than a logical helper name.
The twin changes only the initializer3 to0 and must reject the domain fact.

Matching pass/fail pins are under `tests/omega/{pass,fail}/{calls,domains}/`.
The actual fixed-base/current-producer captures and original failures are
retained in `build/native-binding-evidence`; these controls do not claim audio
hardware success or close the parent native-binding requirement.
