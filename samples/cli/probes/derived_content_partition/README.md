# Derived content partition

One checked interval equation consumes the parent and derives two child
qualifications at their formation, without a parent destructor or an issuer.
The children keep their construction identities and inherit the parent's
content lineage. The program checks both intervals and cleans up both children.

This is the executable prerequisite for Cathedral's retained-backing allocator,
not a physical allocator or a claim that these coordinates own memory. Its
acceptance is `omega run --both main.omg` with native and Terminal exit 0.
`repeated/main.omg` splits a child again while retaining its sibling, checks
all three intervals, and retires all three claims.
Both programs must agree between native and Terminal execution. These interval
programs establish content transfer, not physical allocation or resident access.

Invalid controls live under `tests/omega/fail/core/owned_content_partition_*`
and `tests/omega/fail/core/content_transform_nested_*`: a borrowed parent,
reused parent, premature child authority, absent theorem, or lost nested
custody must not become a valid transformation. An explicit qualification cast
inside the result construction still owes its own earlier authority.
