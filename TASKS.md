Served so far: a domain with no cleanup, operator, alias, parent or parameter, with or without an access policy or establishment routes, on a scalar or a data carrier; with predicates on a scalar carrier, and on a data carrier where the qualified type is a field's (its clauses join the owning type's, `self.reading.value > 0`). A domain's predicates are ordinary contracts of the place declared in it (`read/predicates.rs`): entry `requires` of a formal, `ensures` of a result, `requires` clauses of the data type that declares a field (so its stores, zero establishment and invariant windows are the data type's), and for `value as +D` the invariant of the block the cast continues in, which the cast's edge owes (`lower/control.rs`, `prove_membership`). Every site that reads inside a data value or changes one asks one owner (`read/authority.rs`, `authorized`, `opened`, `written_through`): a domain with no policy restricts neither axis, and a domain with a policy admits no reader or writer until a pass evaluates its plan, so a governed value is passed, lent, stored in a field of its exact type and moved out again, and nothing else. `-D` is served only for a domain anyone may add again. A record with a field in a domain that must be established is gated: `let x: T;` of it is refused (`zero_established`), and a literal supplies every field at its exact type. The parser has no `..` yet; when it arrives it meets the same gate. `+D` is judged in `read/casts.rs`, `issue`, the one owner of that judgment, from `DomainDefinition::issuance()`: anyone adds a domain with no restriction, an exact-machine route adds its routed domain and no other machine does, and a domain with a policy admits no issuer until a pass evaluates its plan (a refusal now, a rejection after). Next, in order: (b) predicates on a data carrier outside a field (`read/predicates.rs`): the cast's proof over the operand's fields, a formal's entry clauses over its fields, a record result's guarantee, and a window for a partial write to a local or formal that carries the domain itself, each refused by name today; a borrowed formal, a case payload and an array element in a domain with predicates, each refused by name; follow a requirement route to the invocation through that requirement; (d) child domains, aliases and indexed families in a type; have the invoker establish the entry parameter's default domain, then migrate the `established by` declarations and delete that clause.

- **DEFAULT-SYNCHRONIZATION-CHECKING.** Implement [mandatory synchronization checking](wiki/spec/language/concurrency.md#default-admission-obligation). The current effects/carry, call-dependency and activation plans do not supply the retained protocol contracts or closed-composition deadlock judgment.
  - First deliver an executable lock provider and guard with compact symbolic resource/transition summaries, inference through helpers/generic wrappers, checked implementation correspondence and publication through ordinary Psi. Implement the [first required automatic floor](wiki/spec/language/concurrency.md#first-required-automatic-floor) for known nonrecursive exclusive locks, finite control/contract summaries and already-established local progress/context evidence; it passes under the default proof policy and `--floor-only` without an extra synchronization-search allowance. Check exact aliases, acquisition order and actual callback/preemption relationships; reuse ownership and installed-root identities. Richer modes and protocols need additional evidence rather than being silently included in that first floor.
  - Then compose task completion with worker/queue/buffer/execution-lease capacity, followed by standard communication protocols, initialization, teardown and replacement. Preserve atomic registration/release/reacquisition and outcome behavior; a timeout needs a runnable handler. Unsupported synchronization refuses the affected composition, never an unqualified deadlock-free verdict.
  - Boundary witnesses use separately compiled packages without implementation bodies. Two valid conditional contracts, A completes if B completes and B completes if A completes, cannot justify one another when wired into mutual waiting; a checked cyclic request/reply protocol with a valid initial state passes. A replacement that waits for E before releasing L cannot reuse a contract that releases L before waiting for E merely because signatures and ordinary effect rows match. A runtime callable with no usable synchronization contract remains unknown even when `blocks`/`suspends` are absent.
  - Invalid compositions reject before execution. Accepted witnesses exercise controlled contention, actual release/wakeup and relevant reentry with an appropriate deterministic scheduling or installation harness, not only uncontended successful calls. A timeout bounds a failed test; avoiding it is not proof of deadlock freedom. These focused execution witnesses do not require interleaving exploration during ordinary compilation.
