# not here, and on a row here that it no longer finds; delete stale rows.
#

# Reasons:
#   noise    how a family talks to its OS (layout offsets, flag words, access
#            masks, helper bounds, OS limits); the call means the same thing.
#   allowed  a difference the portable contract permits, such as Unsupported.
#   known    a real divergence; the note is the exact OWNER_CODE_CONCERNS.md
#            heading that queues it. Fixing it removes the heading and the rows.
