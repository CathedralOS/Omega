MATHEMATICAL CONTRACT
address(r,c) = offset + r*row_stride + c*column_stride, checked in u64.
transpose swaps extents and strides, retaining offset. Double transpose identity.
submatrix checks origin <= extents, then requested extent <= parent-origin;
nonempty offset is address(first_row,first_column), strides retained.
For an empty subview retain the admitted parent offset: no fictitious corner
address or overflow evaluation. Preserve requested zero extents, including 0xN
and Nx0; transpose swaps them. (Requested canonical convention for NF01 review.)
reshape requires equal checked element counts and contiguous column-major logical
order, including singleton/empty degeneracies under NF01 rules. Padded 2x3
(strides 1,3) cannot reshape without an explicit copy. Same-shape reshape of
padded input is also rejected under a contiguous-only contract.
Mutable transforms revalidate under NF01 restricted injectivity policy; readonly
arbitrary admitted mappings may alias. No descriptor licenses a backing borrow.
Adjoint swaps extents/strides and lazily conjugates reads. Re-adjoint toggles
conjugated and transposes again, so double adjoint restores original values.
Backing bounds are rechecked against the separately supplied slice each time.
Any failed try call leaves caller backing unchanged; no trap-based error masking.
